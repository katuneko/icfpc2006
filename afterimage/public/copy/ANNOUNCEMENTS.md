### Long announcement

At midnight, a city receives a complete record of the disasters that will
happen tomorrow. You join the municipal Continuity Desk with one narrow
instruction: change as little as possible.

Afterimage is a clean-room programming-puzzle world built around one causal
spine and eight different kinds of reasoning. Implement a compact evaluator;
repair incident cascades; reconcile incompatible logs; write bounded stream
processors; reconstruct maps; preserve information across views; synthesize
local policies; and finally audit the city's claim that history is unique.

The public set contains 75 scored cases worth 10,000 nominal points. It runs
offline with Python 3.11+, requires no third-party package, and ships one
immutable puzzle bundle with presentation in English, Japanese, Simplified
Chinese, and German.
