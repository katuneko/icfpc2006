# Afterimage public brand guide

## Core idea

The identity expresses two safe causal histories becoming one official public
record. It should feel calm, procedural, exact, and faintly unsettling. It is
not hacker fiction, disaster spectacle, or neon cyberpunk.

## Naming

- Primary: **Afterimage: The Counterfactual City**
- Short: **Afterimage**
- Japanese editorial title: **残像都市――起きなかった明日の監査記録**
- Simplified Chinese editorial title: **余像：反事实之城**
- German editorial title: **Afterimage: Die kontrafaktische Stadt**

Keep `Afterimage` in Latin characters in the primary logo. Localized titles
are editorial lockups, not separate brands.

## Mark

The cyan and coral paths are distinct histories. The amber ring is the single
published record. Do not redraw the paths as arrows, circuit traces, or a
letter A. Do not rotate, outline, add glow, or place the full-color mark on a
busy region without a midnight backing field.

Use `wordmark-light.svg` on midnight or imagery and `wordmark-dark.svg` on
paper. At small sizes use `mark.svg` or `favicon.svg`.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight | `#08131f` | Primary background |
| Slate | `#172a38` | Panels and dividers |
| Paper | `#e8e3d5` | Archival surfaces |
| Paper bright | `#f7f4eb` | Primary text on dark |
| Causal cyan | `#63c7d8` | One history, links, focus |
| Signal amber | `#f0ad4e` | Public record and calls to action |
| Alternate coral | `#e87855` | The other history; use sparingly |

Never communicate validity with cyan versus coral: both paths can be safe.

## Typography

Use Noto Sans across all four launch languages and Noto Sans Mono for case
IDs, commands, hashes, and receipts. System fallbacks are acceptable on the
web. Avoid distressed, terminal-green, typewriter, or pseudo-futurist fonts.

## Photography and illustration

Prefer physical city models, municipal maps, archival paper, frosted layers,
coastal night light, and restrained route overlays. Avoid recognizable real
cities, injured people, emergency spectacle, protected humanitarian emblems,
binary rain, circuit-board skylines, and imagery that implies one path is the
evil path.

## Voice

Use precise, short sentences. State what the participant builds and discovers.
Do not call Continuity evil, promise that players will save the world, or
describe the final construction in enough detail to solve it.

Preferred launch line:

> Build a causal evaluator. Debug tomorrow.

Long pitch:

> Build a causal evaluator. Use it to debug tomorrow. Discover who decided
> that tomorrow was allowed to have only one history.

## Accessibility

- Maintain at least WCAG AA contrast for body text.
- Never encode the two histories by color alone; retain path shape or labels.
- Use the supplied alt text instead of describing the image as a disaster.
- Do not place essential copy inside generated imagery.
